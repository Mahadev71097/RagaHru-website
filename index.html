Ask on WhatsApp
How do we order a design?

Tap Enquire under the design you like. WhatsApp opens with a message that already names the design, such as HN01, so you only need to add your details and send it.

What do you need from us?

Your names, your wedding dates and your venues. If the design shows photographs, send the ones you’d like to use.

How soon will it be ready?

Your website goes live within 24 hours of receiving your details.

How do our guests open it?

It’s a link. Share it on WhatsApp, Instagram or by text, and it opens in the phone’s browser. There’s nothing for guests to download or install.

Can the invitation be in our language?

Designs marked Languages show your invitation in more than one language, and guests switch between them with a tap: English and Kannada, for example.

Can guests reply to the invitation?

Yes, on designs marked RSVP. Guests tell you they’re coming from the website itself.
